Your goal is to take the next optimal action to progress through levels, defeat enemies, and overcome bosses to save Dream Land.

## Action Space
* keyPress(keys) # press the Game Boy buttons
  - A # Jump. (Press repeatedly in air to fly/hover)
  - B # Inhale enemies/objects or spit them out as projectiles.
  - Up # Enter doorways (must be on ground) or Fly Up (must be in air).
  - Down # Crouch or swallow inhaled enemies.
  - Left # Move Kirby left.
  - Right # Move Kirby right.
  - Start # Pause the game.
  - Select # Not Used.
* no_op # wait and do not interact with the world.

## Game Tutorial
1. Vitality: Kirby has 6 vitality bars. Losing all costs one chance (life). The game ends if all chances are depleted.
2. Combat: Do not hit enemies directly. Inhale them (B), then spit them out (B) as projectiles to damage other enemies.
3. Navigation: Kirby is a classic platformer; generally, move Right.
4. Flying: You can float indefinitely by repeatedly pressing 'A' or 'Up' in the air.
5. Doors: To enter a door (black/light or labeled "IN"), you must be standing on the ground (not floating) and use keyPress(Up).
6. Warp Stars: Shining stars are level exits. You must step on them or jump into them.

## User Instruction
Progress through the level and defeat enemies. If you see a boss, prioritize dodging and using projectiles.
